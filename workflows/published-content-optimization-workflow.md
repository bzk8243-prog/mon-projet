# Published Content Optimization Workflow (Lonage AI SEO System)

**Status:** Draft for execution (Do Not Publish)  
**Scope:** Existing indexed Lonage articles only  
**Primary Objective:** Improve performance without changing URL slugs or breaking existing rankings

---

## Workflow Structure

### Phase 0 — Intake & Safeguards (Pre-Optimization)
**Purpose:** Protect existing rankings before making changes.

1. Record baseline data (before edits):
   - Current rankings (primary + secondary keywords)
   - Organic clicks, impressions, CTR
   - Average position
   - Current Rank Math score
   - Current word count and update date
2. Confirm guardrails:
   - Keep existing URL/slug unchanged
   - Preserve strongest ranking sections unless factually outdated
   - Avoid deleting valuable query-matching headings
3. Define optimization target set:
   - 1 primary keyword
   - 3–6 secondary/supporting queries
   - Search intent classification (informational/commercial comparison/transactional assist)

**Output:** Baseline snapshot + optimization brief.

---

### Phase 1 — Existing Article Audit
**Purpose:** Build a full picture of current content performance and structure.

#### Audit Inputs
- Google Search Console query/page data
- Analytics engagement signals (time on page, bounce/engaged sessions)
- Existing article draft in WordPress/Markdown
- SERP snapshot for target keyword cluster

#### Audit Checks
- Intent match quality (does article satisfy dominant SERP intent?)
- Header map quality (H1/H2/H3 hierarchy)
- Thin or repetitive sections
- Missing evidence/examples/data points
- Outdated claims, broken links, outdated product references
- Cannibalization risk with other Lonage pages

**Output:** Audit report with *Keep / Improve / Remove / Expand* labels per section.

---

### Phase 2 — SEO Weakness Detection
**Purpose:** Identify on-page weaknesses that suppress ranking growth.

#### Detect Weaknesses
1. **Keyword mapping issues**
   - Primary keyword absent/weak in title, intro, H2s
   - Secondary entities underused
2. **SERP feature gaps**
   - No FAQ-ready blocks
   - No concise answer snippets for featured snippets
3. **Topical incompleteness**
   - Missing subtopics present across top-ranking competitor pages
4. **On-page friction**
   - Long intro with delayed value delivery
   - Poor scannability (large text walls)

**Output:** Prioritized SEO gap list (High / Medium / Low impact).

---

### Phase 3 — EEAT Gap Analysis
**Purpose:** Strengthen Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust.

#### EEAT Review Framework
- **Experience:** Real-world usage context, practical examples, “when to use/avoid” guidance
- **Expertise:** Evidence-backed explanations, medically/technically accurate language
- **Authoritativeness:** Internal references to pillar pages and cluster depth
- **Trust:** Clear claims, transparent affiliate disclosures, balanced product framing

#### Required EEAT Inserts
- “Reviewed/updated on” date
- Expert-reviewed context where applicable
- Source-backed factual statements
- Risk notes/limitations where relevant

**Output:** EEAT improvement block list mapped to exact article sections.

---

### Phase 4 — Internal Linking Analysis
**Purpose:** Improve crawl pathways, topical depth, and user journey.

#### Internal Linking Rules
1. Add links from article → parent pillar + 2–4 sibling cluster pages.
2. Add reciprocal links from relevant sibling pages (if in scope).
3. Use descriptive anchors (avoid repetitive exact-match spam).
4. Place links contextually near decision moments, definitions, and next-step sections.
5. Ensure no orphaning and no overlinking in a single paragraph.

#### Link Distribution Target
- Intro/body: 1 strategic internal link
- Core sections: 4–8 contextual links total (based on length)
- FAQ: 1–2 links to deeper guidance

**Output:** Internal link insertion map + anchor text plan.

---

### Phase 5 — FAQ Improvement Process
**Purpose:** Capture long-tail intent and increase SERP visibility.

#### FAQ Upgrade Method
1. Extract real questions from:
   - Search Console queries
   - People Also Ask patterns
   - On-site support/comments data
2. Build FAQ set:
   - 5–8 concise, non-duplicative questions
   - Answer each in 40–80 words (direct first sentence)
3. Ensure intent coverage mix:
   - Clarification question
   - Comparison question
   - Safety/side-effect question (if relevant)
   - Practical “how/when” question
4. Validate schema readiness and readability.

**Output:** Optimized FAQ block aligned to ranking queries.

---

### Phase 6 — Rank Math Optimization
**Purpose:** Improve plugin-level on-page scoring while preserving natural quality.

#### Rank Math Target Checklist
- Focus keyword in SEO title
- Focus keyword near start of meta description
- Focus keyword in first paragraph
- Focus keyword in at least one H2/H3
- URL unchanged (no slug edits unless critical and approved)
- Adequate internal/external links
- Image alt text relevance
- Content length appropriate to SERP depth

**Target Score:** 85+ (without keyword stuffing)

**Output:** Rank Math pass report with exceptions logged.

---

### Phase 7 — Readability Optimization
**Purpose:** Increase comprehension, retention, and engagement.

#### Readability Rules
- Average sentence length: short-to-medium
- Paragraph length: 2–4 lines typical
- Use bullets for steps/comparisons
- Keep jargon explained at first mention
- Add summary boxes for complex sections
- Remove fluff and repeated phrasing

#### Weak Content Reduction
- Cut generic filler intros
- Merge overlapping sections
- Replace vague advice with actionable guidance

**Output:** Cleaner high-signal draft with better scannability.

---

### Phase 8 — Human Tone Verification
**Purpose:** Ensure content sounds credible, warm, and non-robotic.

#### Tone Validation
- Conversational but expert
- No AI-generic filler patterns
- Brand-consistent voice (Lonage: evidence-led, practical, supportive)
- Strong transitions and narrative flow

**Output:** Tone-approved draft.

---

### Phase 9 — Affiliate Compliance Review
**Purpose:** Improve commercial conversion without trust erosion.

#### Affiliate Positioning Standards
1. Place affiliate mentions only where intent is commercial or decision-support.
2. Prioritize recommendation logic over promotional language.
3. Include “who this is for / not for” framing.
4. Ensure disclosure is visible and compliant.
5. Balance affiliate links with non-commercial educational value.

**Output:** Compliance-reviewed monetization layer.

---

### Phase 10 — Final Publication Checklist (Do Not Publish Step)
**Purpose:** Validate readiness and hand off safely.

#### Final Checklist
- [ ] URL/slug unchanged
- [ ] Baseline data captured
- [ ] SEO weaknesses resolved or documented
- [ ] EEAT blocks inserted
- [ ] Internal links validated (no broken links)
- [ ] FAQ improved and structured
- [ ] Rank Math score meets threshold or rationale logged
- [ ] Readability pass complete
- [ ] Human tone pass complete
- [ ] Affiliate compliance pass complete
- [ ] Final diff reviewed by editor
- [ ] Status marked: **Ready for Manual Publish**

**Output:** Publication-ready draft, queued only (not published).

## Optimization Checklist (Reusable)

Use this checklist for every existing article refresh:

### A) Technical & SEO
- [ ] Preserve URL
- [ ] Verify title/H1 alignment with primary intent
- [ ] Improve meta title for CTR + clarity
- [ ] Improve meta description for value + action
- [ ] Ensure primary keyword placement (natural)
- [ ] Add/refresh internal links
- [ ] Verify external references where needed

### B) Content Quality
- [ ] Add missing subtopics from SERP expectations
- [ ] Remove weak/repetitive sections
- [ ] Strengthen examples and specificity
- [ ] Add concise takeaways per major section

### C) EEAT
- [ ] Add/update factual support
- [ ] Add practical experience cues
- [ ] Include balanced caveats and limitations
- [ ] Confirm trust signals (accuracy, transparency)

### D) FAQ & Structured Readability
- [ ] Add/upgrade 5–8 FAQs from query demand
- [ ] Keep answers concise and direct
- [ ] Improve scan formatting (bullets, short paragraphs)

### E) Monetization & Compliance
- [ ] Ensure natural affiliate placement
- [ ] Confirm disclosure visibility
- [ ] Keep non-commercial educational balance

### F) Final QA
- [ ] Rank Math score target achieved
- [ ] Tone/human quality verified
- [ ] Grammar/spelling pass
- [ ] Ready for manual publish handoff

## Reusable Optimization Process (SOP)

1. **Select article** from refresh queue by highest opportunity (high impressions, low CTR or decaying rank).
2. **Capture baseline metrics** and snapshot existing structure.
3. **Run 10-phase workflow** in order (Audit → Final checklist).
4. **Score article** using system below.
5. **If score < threshold**, iterate weak categories only.
6. **Mark status**:
   - Draft optimized
   - Awaiting editorial review
   - Ready for manual publish

---

## Scoring System

### Category Weights (100 points total)
- SEO Fundamentals: 20
- EEAT Strength: 20
- Internal Linking: 15
- FAQ Quality: 10
- Readability & UX: 15
- CTR Metadata Quality: 10
- Affiliate Compliance & Trust: 10

### Scoring Rubric (per category)
- **0–39% of category points:** Major issues unresolved
- **40–69%:** Partial optimization, important gaps remain
- **70–89%:** Strong optimization with minor improvements possible
- **90–100%:** Publication-ready quality

### Release Thresholds
- **85+ total:** Ready for manual publish queue
- **75–84:** Needs one optimization iteration
- **<75:** Return to full audit and rewrite weak sections

### Priority Flags
- Any category scoring below 60% becomes a **blocking issue** even if total score is 85+.
