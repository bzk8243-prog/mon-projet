# Lonage Article QA Checklist

## SEO
- Main keyword in title
- Main keyword in introduction
- H2/H3 structure present
- Meta description included
- FAQ section included
- Rank Math friendly structure

## Readability
- Human-written tone
- Natural transitions
- Clear paragraphs
- No robotic AI phrasing

## Internal Linking
- Pillar links added
- Sibling links added
- Cross-cluster links added

## Affiliate
- Product mention near end
- Soft-sell style
- Educational first

## Image
- Photorealistic prompt included
- Consistent Lonage editorial style

## Final Validation
- Grammar checked
- Formatting checked
- SEO title optimized
- CTA included
