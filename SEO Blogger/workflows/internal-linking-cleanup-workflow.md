# Internal Linking Cleanup Workflow

## Goal
Clean and strengthen Lonage internal linking structure before publishing new articles.

## Priority
1. Detect orphan articles
2. Connect every article to its main pillar
3. Connect every pillar to its related articles
4. Add sibling article links inside the same cluster
5. Add contextual cross-cluster links only when relevant
6. Preserve all existing URLs and slugs
7. Do not publish automatically

## Link Types
- Pillar to article
- Article to pillar
- Article to article
- Cluster to article
- Article to related cluster

## Rules
- Do not delete existing URLs
- Do not rename slugs
- Do not force irrelevant links
- Prioritize menopause, fitness, nutrition, sleep, recovery, and mobility clusters
- Use Rank Math internal linking logic

## Output Expected From Codex
- List of orphan articles
- Internal linking map
- Per-article linking recommendations
- Priority order for implementation
- WordPress-safe implementation plan
