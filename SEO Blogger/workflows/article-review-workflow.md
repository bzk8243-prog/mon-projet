# Lonage Article Review Workflow

## Step 1 — Read the article
Review the full article from beginning to end.

## Step 2 — Check brand voice
Use:
knowledge/brand-voice.md

## Step 3 — Check article template
Use:
templates/master-article-template.md

## Step 4 — Check SEO
Use:
qa/article-qa-checklist.md

## Step 5 — Check internal linking
Verify:
- Pillar link
- Sibling links
- Cross-cluster link

## Step 6 — Check affiliate style
Product mention should stay near the end and use soft-sell language.

## Step 7 — Final decision
Mark article as:
- Ready to publish
- Needs revision
- Not ready
