# Lonage Global Topical Authority Architecture

This document refactors Lonage from a menopause-first SEO structure into a unified topical authority system where menopause is one ecosystem among ten. It is designed to scale editorially while preserving existing URLs, slugs, and category taxonomy.

## Global Architecture Rules
- Keep all published/indexed URLs and slugs unchanged.
- Preserve current category names and parent-child taxonomy.
- Grow authority with net-new pages, cluster expansion, and internal linking.
- Route every article through exactly one **primary ecosystem** and up to two **secondary ecosystems** for cross-linking.
- Maintain a shared entity library so terminology is consistent across all ecosystems.

## 1) Menopause Ecosystem

### 1.1 Authority map
- **Core intent:** menopause symptom clarity + practical management.
- **Topical zones:** perimenopause, symptom management, training adaptation, sleep disruption, metabolism changes, mood/cognition.
- **Cross-ecosystem gateways:** Sleep, Nutrition, Fitness, Recovery, Women’s Wellness, Longevity.

### 1.2 Pillars
- Menopause symptoms guide (hub).
- Perimenopause transition guide.
- Lifestyle strategy for menopause (nutrition, movement, recovery).

### 1.3 Clusters
- Vasomotor symptoms (hot flashes/night sweats).
- Menopause sleep and fatigue.
- Brain fog, mood, and focus.
- Weight and body-composition changes.
- Exercise programming in peri/post-menopause.
- Supplement and nutrition support.

### 1.4 Article relationships
- Pillar → cluster explainer → action article (3-tier structure).
- Symptom articles interlink by co-occurrence (e.g., sleep + mood + fatigue).
- Lifecycle pages (peri vs post) link bidirectionally to symptom pages.

### 1.5 Internal linking strategy
- Every menopause cluster article links to 1 menopause pillar + 2 sibling clusters + 1 adjacent ecosystem article.
- Anchor pattern mix: exact match (20%), partial match (50%), contextual natural anchors (30%).
- Add "next-step" blocks at end of articles: symptom → treatment → lifestyle.

### 1.6 Semantic SEO entities
- Menopause, perimenopause, postmenopause, estrogen decline, progesterone, hot flashes, sleep fragmentation, insulin sensitivity, sarcopenia risk.

### 1.7 Affiliate opportunities
- Cooling sleep products, magnesium, protein powders, resistance bands/dumbbells, wearables for sleep/recovery, symptom-tracking apps.

### 1.8 Content scoring rules
- Symptom accuracy and medical safety (30).
- Practical actionability (20).
- Internal link depth and relevance (15).
- Entity coverage completeness (15).
- E-E-A-T signals (expert review, references) (20).

### 1.9 Workflow variations
- Mandatory expert review checkpoint for symptom/intervention claims.
- Higher refresh cadence: every 90 days for top traffic symptom pages.
- Add FAQ schema + medically reviewed snippet block.

## Global Article Relationship Framework (All Ecosystems)
- **Tier 1:** Pillar hubs (broad intent).
- **Tier 2:** Cluster explainers (specific intent).
- **Tier 3:** Action pages (implementation, checklists, plans, product comparisons).
- **Bridge content:** cross-ecosystem translators (e.g., menopause sleep, nutrition for recovery, mobility for longevity).

Each net-new article must define:
1. Primary ecosystem.
2. Parent pillar.
3. Cluster node.
4. Two sibling links.
5. One cross-ecosystem bridge link.
6. One conversion opportunity (affiliate/product/service CTA where appropriate).

## Global Internal Linking Rules
- Minimum 4 contextual internal links per article:
  - 1 upward link (pillar),
  - 2 lateral links (same ecosystem),
  - 1 bridge link (different ecosystem).
- Avoid repeated exact-match anchors in the same article.
- Prioritize links into under-linked strategic pages using monthly orphan/underlinked audits.

## Global Semantic SEO Entity Governance
- Maintain one shared entity glossary with canonical definitions and synonyms.
- Require entity coverage checks during content QA.
- Ensure each article targets:
  - 1 primary entity,
  - 3-5 secondary entities,
  - 2 contextual related entities from a sister ecosystem.

## Global Affiliate Opportunity Model
- Classify opportunities into:
  1. Daily-use products,
  2. Programmatic tools/apps,
  3. Premium diagnostics/services,
  4. Education/program memberships.
- Match affiliate modules to search intent stage (informational vs commercial).
- Enforce trust-first placements (recommendation rationale + alternatives).

## Global Content Scoring System (100 points)
- Topical accuracy and trust: 30
- Actionability and UX structure: 20
- Internal linking contribution: 15
- Semantic entity depth: 15
- Conversion quality (non-spam affiliate fit): 10
- Freshness and maintainability: 10

Publishing threshold:
- 85+ = publish
- 75-84 = revise before publish
- <75 = rewrite required

## Global Workflow Variations
- **Standard workflow:** brief → draft → SEO pass → internal linking pass → QA → publish.
- **Medical-adjacent workflow (menopause/gut/sleep symptoms):** adds expert/compliance review.
- **Commercial workflow (affiliate-heavy pages):** adds intent-match + trust/comparison QA.
- **Refresh workflow:** prioritized by traffic decay, ranking loss, or underlinking.

## Implementation Note
The prior menopause authority model remains intact but is now governed as Ecosystem 1 inside this broader Lonage authority architecture. All future planning, briefs, and internal linking decisions should start from this multi-ecosystem system.
