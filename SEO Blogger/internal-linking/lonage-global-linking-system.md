# Lonage Global Internal Linking System (All Health & Fitness Ecosystems)

## Purpose
Operational linking framework across all site ecosystems while preserving indexed URLs/categories/slugs.

## Core Rules
1. Do not change existing indexed URLs.
2. Do not rename categories/slugs.
3. Add links contextually in body sections.
4. Every article must link to:
   - one parent pillar,
   - 2–4 sibling articles,
   - 1 cross-ecosystem article when relevant.

## Ecosystem Linking Quotas
- Pillar pages: 8–20 internal links (mix of cluster + cross-ecosystem).
- Cluster pages: 3–7 internal links (pillar + siblings + bridge).

## Rank Math Internal Linking Recommendations
- Use natural anchors with mixed phrasing.
- Keep exact-match anchors under control (avoid repetitive use).
- Match linked section intent to destination intent.
- Ensure title/meta/H2 themes align with linked ecosystem journey.

## Cross-Ecosystem Bridge Examples
- Menopause article on sleep disturbance → Sleep pillar + Recovery cluster.
- Fitness beginner guide → Mobility basics + Recovery routine.
- Gut health article on bloating → Nutrition digestion cluster + Women’s wellness stress-gut piece.

## Orphan Detection & Recovery
- Crawl site monthly.
- Flag URLs with 0 contextual inlinks.
- Recover with: 1 pillar + 2 siblings + 1 cross-ecosystem link.
- Re-crawl and mark recovered.
