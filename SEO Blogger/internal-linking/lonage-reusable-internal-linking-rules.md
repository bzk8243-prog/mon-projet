# Reusable Internal Linking Rules (Future Articles)

1. Assign every new article to one primary ecosystem: Menopause, Healthy aging, Fitness, Women's wellness, Recovery, Nutrition, Sleep, Gut health, Mobility, Longevity.
2. Assign one cluster intent (symptom, goal, method, life-stage, or troubleshooting).
3. Before publish, add minimum internal links:
   - 1 parent pillar link
   - 2 sibling links in same ecosystem
   - 1 cross-cluster contextual link
4. Anchor text mix:
   - ~30% exact match max
   - remaining partial-match, semantic, and CTA anchors
5. Location mix:
   - 1 link in intro
   - 1–3 links in body H2 sections
   - 1 link in conclusion/next-step block
6. If no sibling exists, link to category hub and mark topic as “supporting article needed”.
7. Recheck after 30/90 days in Rank Math and update links based on impressions + CTR pages.
